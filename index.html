The problem

If the buyer denies it later, what proof do you have?

01

Your billing software only tracks the bill

It shows what you invoiced. It can't tell you whether the buyer actually received the goods on the terms you both agreed.

02

Trading apps stop once the deal is made

They help you find the buyer. After that, the deal moves to phone and WhatsApp like it always did — no proof either side can point to.

03

A GST invoice proves tax, not acceptance

Your e-invoice is valid for the government. It says nothing about whether the buyer agreed the goods reached them as promised.

Account details you provide at signup (name, company, GSTIN, phone, email), and deal data you enter or that your buyers confirm through Kranti — order, invoice, and acknowledgement records.

Buyer, agent & transporter data

These contacts are entered by the seller organization, not by us. They interact with Kranti only through a link and a single-use verification code — no account is created on their behalf.

How it's used

Solely to operate the acknowledgement pipeline: generating links, sending single-use verification codes, sealing and archiving receipts, and account support. We do not sell data to third parties.
